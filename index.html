Geographical Overview

Location & Landscape

🗺️
Border Location

Located in northwest Iraq in the Sindi plain (lat 37.14, long 42.7). Positioned 10 km from Turkey's border and 30 km from Syria's border.

🏔️
Topography

Features rugged mountain trails, deep valleys, and rich agricultural plains including Sindi and Slevani plains.

🌊
Khabur River

Flows through the city center, meeting the Hezel River 6 km downstream to form the Iraq-Turkey border. Rich in oil and water springs.

Economy & Growth

Projects & Infrastructure

🛃
Ibrahim Khalil Crossing

Vital trade gateway to Turkey and Europe. Trade exports reached over $12.6 Billion in 2023.

✈️
International Airport

Under construction near Zakho-Duhok border with an allocated budget of $12,832,000.

🛣️
Roads & Dams

Paved 108 km of roads and constructed 5 submersible dams along Khabur River (225B IQD budget).

♻️
Recycling Plant

A $23M private recycling facility recycling 80% of local waste and creating 150 jobs.

Culture & Sights

Tourism & Landmarks

🌉
Dalal Bridge

Ancient stone bridge spanning 114 meters long and 4.7 meters wide over the Khabur River.

🏯
Zakho Castle & Bazaar

Historic 13th-century castle converted into a heritage spot. Restored 140-year-old Ottoman Bazaar hosting 140 shops.

🌊
Sharansh Waterfall & Cave

22-meter waterfall 18 km northeast. Nearby Bahiri Cave receives up to 2,000 visitors daily.
